How to play

A reaction game. Moles pop out of their holes for a moment and you have to catch them before they duck back down.

  1. Press Start game to begin the thirty second round.
  2. Click or tap a mole as soon as it appears.
  3. Each hit adds a point. Your score shows at the top left.
  4. The timer counts down at the top right.
  5. When time runs out the round ends and your best score is saved.
